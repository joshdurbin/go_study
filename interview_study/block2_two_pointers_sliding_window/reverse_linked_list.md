PROBLEM: Reverse a Singly Linked List
=====================================
Given the head of a singly linked list, reverse it and return the new head.

Why this block: pointer manipulation problem in the same family as fast/slow.
Three-pointer iteration (prev, cur, next) — memorize the pattern.

Example:
  1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5  ⇒  5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1
  []                  ⇒  []
