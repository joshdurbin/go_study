PROBLEM: Fibonacci
==================
Return the n-th Fibonacci number. F(0)=0, F(1)=1, F(n)=F(n-1)+F(n-2).

Why it's a warm-up: lets you talk about three solution shapes:
  - naive recursion: O(2ⁿ) — exponential
  - memoized recursion: O(n) time, O(n) space
  - iterative two-variable: O(n) time, O(1) space ← preferred

Interviewers often ask you to walk through all three.
