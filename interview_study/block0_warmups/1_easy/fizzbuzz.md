PROBLEM: FizzBuzz
=================
Print numbers 1..n. For multiples of 3 print "Fizz", multiples of 5 print "Buzz",
multiples of both print "FizzBuzz".

Why it's a warm-up: tests basic loop, modulo, control flow, and string handling.
Common follow-up: generalize so callers can supply (divisor, label) pairs.

Example:
  n=15 → 1 2 Fizz 4 Buzz Fizz 7 8 Fizz Buzz 11 Fizz 13 14 FizzBuzz
